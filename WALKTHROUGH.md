# Three-minute walkthrough script and shot list

## Script

**0:00–0:25 — Input and trust**
“Velora starts with two CRM exports. I can upload them or use the bundled reviewer sample, then analyze the complete account book as one job. Raw files stay in this browser session; AI receives only validated account summaries.”

**0:25–0:50 — Full-book ranking**
Click **Analyze account book** and land on the VP view. “All 285 eligible organizations are ranked. Choose **Rep A** to see that rep’s complete read-only book. Scoring controls and data-management tools remain VP-only.”

**0:50–1:20 — Explain the first rank**  
Open the first account. “The drawer shows priority, account and intent scores, band, factor inputs, engagement timeline, data status, warnings, recommended action, why now, urgency, call angle, and confidence. Unknown values stay unknown.”

**1:20–1:45 — Business controls**  
Close the drawer and move the Intent slider. “Leadership can tune this week’s focus. The other two weights redistribute proportionally so the total always remains 100%, and the list reranks immediately. Reset restores the published 55/30/15 strategy.”

**1:45–2:15 — Data quality and refresh**  
Open **Review issues**. “No problematic input disappears: each flag includes source evidence and a suggested CRM correction. High-severity identity or owner problems are held out; usable warnings remain visible.” Close it and open **Replace account book**. “Both exports are analyzed together, and a bad pair leaves the current ranking unchanged.”

**2:15–2:35 — Handoff**  
Click **Export full ranking**. “The CSV contains every eligible organization, global and owner ranks, aliases, component scores, confidence, reasons, warnings, active weights, and as-of date—enough to reproduce and audit the call plan.”

**2:35–3:00 — Optional AI and close**  
Open **Analysis status**. “The model interprets the full book in bounded internal batches and returns only why now, action, urgency, call angle, and confidence. It cannot return scores or ranks. Coverage shows what came from AI versus deterministic fallback, and any failed batch remains actionable.”

## Shot list

1. 1440×900 intake view with both upload cards, sample shortcut, trust panel, and Velora logo.
2. Analysis progress followed by the 285-row VP ranking and Rep A’s complete account book.
3. First-account drawer with scores, recommendation, timeline, and warnings.
4. Slider change followed by Reset.
5. Review queue filtered to ARR, showing evidence and suggested correction.
6. Replacement dialog: invalid rejection, then valid account-book analysis.
7. Full-ranking CSV download.
8. AI-interpreted action badge; optionally show deterministic fallback as a quick cut.

Record at 1440×900 with browser zoom at 100%. Keep cursor movement deliberate and avoid scrolling during the opening 25 seconds.
