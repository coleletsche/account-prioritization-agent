# Three-minute walkthrough script and shot list

## Script

**0:00–0:25 — Outcome and trust**  
“This is Velora’s daily sales-prioritization agent. It turns two CRM exports into a global Top 25 and a Top 10 for each SDR. Scores, P0–P3 bands, validation, and policy are deterministic; AI only interprets the result.”

**0:25–0:50 — SDR workflow**  
Choose **Rep A** from the persona selector. “The rep gets a read-only daily Top 10 with priority, recent signals, why now, and recommended action. Scoring controls and data-management tools remain VP-only.”

**0:50–1:20 — Explain the first rank**  
Open the first account. “The drawer shows priority, account and intent scores, band, factor inputs, engagement timeline, data status, warnings, recommended action, why now, urgency, call angle, and confidence. Unknown values stay unknown.”

**1:20–1:45 — Business controls**  
Close the drawer and move the Intent slider. “Leadership can tune this week’s focus. The other two weights redistribute proportionally so the total always remains 100%, and the list reranks immediately. Reset restores the published 55/30/15 strategy.”

**1:45–2:15 — Data quality and refresh**  
Open **Review issues**. “No problematic input disappears: each flag includes source evidence and a suggested CRM correction. High-severity identity or owner problems are held out; usable warnings lower confidence but remain visible.” Close it and open **Refresh data**. “Both exports are validated together. A bad file leaves the current ranking unchanged; a good pair shows a counts preview before apply. Files stay in this browser tab.”

**2:15–2:35 — Handoff**  
Click **Export full ranking**. “The CSV contains every eligible organization, global and owner ranks, aliases, component scores, confidence, reasons, warnings, active weights, and as-of date—enough to reproduce and audit the call plan.”

**2:35–3:00 — Optional AI and close**  
Click **Interpret daily queues**. “The model receives at most 40 validated summaries and returns only why now, action, urgency, call angle, and confidence. It cannot return scores or ranks. Deterministic post-model policy still blocks bad identity/data and suppression. Any failure preserves the deterministic action plan.”

## Shot list

1. 1440×900 landing view with hero, freshness warning, overview cards, and Velora logo.
2. Persona selector and Rep A’s ten-row daily queue.
3. First-account drawer with scores, recommendation, timeline, and warnings.
4. Slider change followed by Reset.
5. Review queue filtered to ARR, showing evidence and suggested correction.
6. Refresh dialog: invalid rejection, then valid 300/360/286 preview.
7. Full-ranking CSV download.
8. AI-interpreted action badge; optionally show deterministic fallback as a quick cut.

Record at 1440×900 with browser zoom at 100%. Keep cursor movement deliberate and avoid scrolling during the opening 25 seconds.
