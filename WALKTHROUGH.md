# Three-minute walkthrough script and shot list

## Script

**0:00–0:25 — Input and trust**
“Velora starts with two CRM exports. I can upload them or use the bundled reviewer sample, then analyze the complete account book as one job. AI plans are optional: this checkbox generates all of them up front, while leaving it off creates only the deterministic ranking.”

**0:25–0:50 — Full-book ranking**
Click **Analyze account book** and land on the VP view. “All 285 eligible organizations are ranked. Choose **Rep A** to see that rep’s complete read-only book. Scoring controls and data-management tools remain VP-only.”

**0:50–1:20 — Explain the first rank**  
Click **Generate AI plan** on the first row, then open it. “The ranking existed before AI. This explicit action adds a grounded Why now, recommended action, and call plan without changing the score or rank. The drawer preserves the deterministic factors, timeline, and warnings alongside the generated plan.”

**1:20–1:45 — Business controls**  
Close the drawer and move the Intent slider. “Leadership can tune this week’s focus. The other two weights redistribute proportionally so the total always remains 100%, and the list reranks immediately. Reset restores the published 55/30/15 strategy.”

**1:45–2:15 — Data reconciliation and refresh**
Open **Reconcile data**. “No problematic input disappears. Warnings are grouped by source record, and the VP can correct the working CSV or JSON row. Save and rescore reruns the same deterministic pipeline; a warning leaves only when validation passes.” Correct the held-out sample identity and show it enter the ranking, then download the corrected CSV. “The original local file is untouched, and a bad replacement pair still leaves the current ranking unchanged.”

**2:15–2:35 — Handoff**  
Click **Export full ranking**. “The CSV contains every eligible organization, global and owner ranks, aliases, component scores, confidence, reasons, warnings, active weights, and as-of date—enough to reproduce and audit the call plan.”

**2:35–3:00 — Optional AI and close**  
Open **AI plan status**. “Generate all plans handles the full book in bounded batches and returns only Why now, action, urgency, call plan, and confidence. Coverage distinguishes generated from not generated. A failed plan stays empty and retryable—deterministic copy is never presented as AI interpretation.”

## Shot list

1. 1440×900 intake view with both upload cards, sample shortcut, trust panel, and Velora logo.
2. Analysis progress followed by the 285-row VP ranking and Rep A’s complete account book.
3. First row before and after **Generate AI plan**, followed by its drawer.
4. Slider change followed by Reset.
5. Data reconciliation workspace: correct the held-out identity, show 286 eligible accounts, and download the corrected CSV.
6. Replacement dialog: invalid rejection, then valid account-book analysis.
7. Full-ranking CSV download.
8. AI plan status showing generated and not-generated coverage.

Record at 1440×900 with browser zoom at 100%. Keep cursor movement deliberate and avoid scrolling during the opening 25 seconds.
